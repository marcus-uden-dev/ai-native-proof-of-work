# Household Budget App Go-To-Market Strategy

Last updated: 2026-05-11
Status: Deferred hypothesis

## Project Scope

Project: household budget app.

Role in portfolio: supporting proof point for financial-product UX, household domain modeling, shared access, and test-backed execution.

## Initial Audience

Likely early reviewers:

- households that already budget together
- spreadsheet-heavy personal finance users
- people who need shared account visibility and liquidity forecasting

## Distribution Channels

| Channel | Why It Might Work | Risk | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Private sample-data walkthroughs | Tests household workflow comprehension safely | Manual and narrow | Planned |
| Personal finance power-user feedback | Relevant pain points | Privacy/trust concerns | Planned |
| Portfolio case study | Shows domain modeling and testing | Does not validate buyer demand | Active |
| Public launch | Could test demand | Premature before privacy/security validation | Deferred |

## GTM Principle

Do not sell "another budgeting dashboard."

Sell:

- shared household money workflows
- import and review discipline
- liquidity forecasting
- goals and recurring expense planning
- privacy/security-aware household access

## Validation Plan

1. Verify a privacy-safe demo path with sample data.
2. Test whether users understand the shared-household wedge.
3. Identify whether import review or forecasting creates the strongest pull.
4. Defer public launch until security and privacy boundaries are validated.
