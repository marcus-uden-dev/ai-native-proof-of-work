# Household Budget App Business Model

Last updated: 2026-05-11
Status: Hypothesis / Not validated

## Project Scope

Project: household budget app.

Role in portfolio: supporting proof point for financial-product UX, household domain modeling, shared access, and test-backed execution.

## Possible Customer Segments

| Segment | Problem | Willingness to Pay | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shared households | Need shared budget visibility and forecasting | Unknown | Natural product fit |
| Couples / families | Need roles, shared accounts, and review workflows | Unknown | Privacy/trust is central |
| Personal finance power users | Need imports, goals, and forecasting | Possible | Competitive market |

## Business Model Options

| Model | Pros | Cons | Best Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Household subscription | Clear buyer unit | Competitive consumer finance market | Shared households |
| One-time purchase | Simple and privacy-friendly | Harder to sustain support | Personal finance power users |
| Premium imports/forecasting | Ties price to advanced value | Requires strong UX proof | Heavy users |
| Local/self-hosted plus services | Trust-friendly | Smaller market | Technical/privacy-focused users |

## Current Hypothesis

If commercialized, the app would likely need a privacy-first personal finance subscription model. The proof point is currently stronger as product/domain execution evidence than as a validated business.

## Rejected or Deferred Models

| Model | Status | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Financial advice product | Deferred | Regulatory and trust complexity |
| Bank-data aggregation product | Deferred | Integration and compliance burden |
| B2B finance tool | Deferred | Different buyer and workflow |
