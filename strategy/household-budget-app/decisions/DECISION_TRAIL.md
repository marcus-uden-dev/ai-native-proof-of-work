## Decisions

### 2026-05-11 — Treat household budgeting as shared-domain modeling evidence

#### Context

The app demonstrates household-scoped data, imports, transactions, recurring expenses, forecasting, goals, and security boundaries. Commercial validation is not yet available.

#### Options Considered

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Treat household budget app as lead product strategy | Strong domain-modeling evidence | Less connected to career/recruiter workflow than job-agent |
| Treat it as supporting proof point | Shows range and reliability without diluting focus | Less commercial detail for the app |
| Defer strategy entirely | Avoids weak market claims | Hides useful financial-product UX and security evidence |

#### Tradeoffs

Supporting positioning preserves the job-agent lead while still showing careful domain modeling, privacy/security thinking, and test-backed execution.

#### Decision

Position the household budget app as a supporting proof point for domain modeling, financial-product UX, privacy/security thinking, and test-backed execution.
