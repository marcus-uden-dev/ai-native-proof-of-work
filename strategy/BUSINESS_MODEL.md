# Business Model

Last updated: 2026-05-08  
Status: Hypothesis / Not validated

## Possible Customer Segments

| Segment | Problem | Willingness to Pay | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Job seekers | Need better applications and proof of work | Unknown | Strong initial relevance |
| Career switchers | Need credibility without traditional credentials | Unknown | Strong proof-of-work fit |
| Consultants | Need reusable proof and case studies | Possible | Higher willingness if tied to revenue |
| Small teams | Need workflow documentation and automation | Possible | Requires B2B positioning |
| AI-native agencies | Need reusable operating systems | Possible | More advanced buyer |

## Business Model Options

| Model | Pros | Cons | Best Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free + Pro | Easy adoption | Conversion risk | B2C |
| Monthly subscription | Predictable revenue | Churn risk | Power users |
| One-time setup | Clear value | Less recurring revenue | Consultants/job seekers |
| Usage-based | Aligns cost with usage | Harder to explain | Automation-heavy workflows |
| B2B/team license | Higher revenue | Longer sales cycle | Teams/agencies |

## Current Hypothesis

The most realistic early model is a one-time setup or guided implementation package for power users, consultants, or job seekers who already produce valuable work but struggle to package it.

## Rejected or Deferred Models

| Model | Status | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Fully automated job agent SaaS | Deferred | Too broad for early validation |
| Public social posting automation | Deferred | Platform and trust risk |
| Raw data ingestion product | Deferred | Privacy and complexity risk |

## Validation Needed

- Will users pay for clearer proof-of-work packaging?
- Which artifact creates the most value: CV bullet, recruiter brief, portfolio case study, or demo script?
- Is the strongest market job seekers, consultants, or AI-native teams?
