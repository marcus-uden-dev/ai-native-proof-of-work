# PKM Business Model

Last updated: 2026-05-11
Status: Hypothesis / Not validated

## Project Scope

Project: PKM.

Role in portfolio: supporting proof point for knowledge workflow design.

## Possible Customer Segments

| Segment | Problem | Willingness to Pay | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Individual knowledge workers | Need better capture, retrieval, and review | Unknown | Most natural early segment |
| Builders / founders | Need source memory for product and research work | Unknown | Strong workflow fit |
| Small teams | Need shared knowledge workflows | Possible | Requires collaboration scope |

## Business Model Options

| Model | Pros | Cons | Best Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Individual subscription | Simple and familiar | Requires sustained daily value | Power users |
| One-time setup | Concrete for messy existing systems | Less scalable | Builders with existing source sprawl |
| Team license | Higher value | Requires collaboration/admin scope | Small teams |
| Open/self-hosted plus services | Trust-friendly | Harder to monetize directly | Technical users |

## Current Hypothesis

If commercialized, PKM likely starts as an individual power-user tool. Team or small-business models should wait until the personal workflow is demonstrably reliable.

## Rejected or Deferred Models

| Model | Status | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise knowledge base | Deferred | Too broad before daily-use validation |
| Public feed/social product | Deferred | Different product and trust model |
| Fully automated research assistant | Deferred | Requires stronger source-quality guarantees |
