## Decisions

### 2026-05-11 — Treat PKM as supporting strategy evidence until daily-use value is validated

#### Context

PKM shows knowledge workflow design, ingestion, search, learning, browser capture, and feature lifecycle discipline, but external product value is not yet validated.

#### Options Considered

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Treat PKM as lead product strategy | Shows breadth beyond job-agent | Dilutes the stronger career-domain proof point |
| Treat PKM as supporting proof point | Keeps portfolio focus clear | Less commercial detail for PKM |
| Defer PKM strategy entirely | Avoids weak claims | Hides useful knowledge-workflow evidence |

#### Tradeoffs

Supporting positioning preserves the job-agent lead while still showing transferable product and workflow thinking.

#### Decision

Position PKM as a supporting proof point rather than the lead product strategy.

#### Evidence

See [PKM Case Study](../../../case-studies/PKM_CASE_STUDY.md).

#### Open Questions

- Which PKM workflow is strongest enough for a sanitized demo?
- Does the product have a commercial path or mainly demonstrate workflow thinking?

#### Next Action

Verify and summarize one end-to-end daily-use path.
