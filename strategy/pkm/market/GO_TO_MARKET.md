# PKM Go-To-Market Strategy

Last updated: 2026-05-11
Status: Deferred hypothesis

## Project Scope

Project: PKM.

Role in portfolio: supporting proof point for knowledge workflow design.

## Initial Audience

Likely early reviewers:

- personal knowledge-management power users
- builders with source-heavy research workflows
- people who maintain notes but struggle to retrieve and act on them

## Distribution Channels

| Channel | Why It Might Work | Risk | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Private demos | Shows workflow value directly | Manual and narrow | Planned |
| PKM/community feedback | Relevant early audience | Can over-index on niche preferences | Planned |
| Builder notes/case study | Demonstrates source-heavy workflow | Requires privacy-safe examples | Planned |
| GitHub portfolio sharing | Useful for recruiter proof | Does not validate buyer demand | Active |

## GTM Principle

Do not sell "another notes app."

Sell:

- capture-to-retrieval workflow
- learning and review loops
- source quality and prioritization
- feature lifecycle discipline

## Validation Plan

1. Verify the daily capture, search, learning, and browser-capture workflow.
2. Identify one sanitized demo path.
3. Ask users which part feels differentiated from existing tools.
4. Defer public launch until operational blockers are cleared.
