# PKM Value Proposition

Last updated: 2026-05-11
Status: Supporting project hypothesis / Internal evidence summarized

## Project Scope

Project: PKM.

Role in portfolio: supporting proof point for knowledge workflow design.

## Core Claim

PKM helps builders and knowledge workers turn scattered notes, links, sources, ideas, and learning material into retrievable, reviewable, and reusable knowledge.

## User Problem

Personal knowledge work often becomes passive storage. Useful material is captured but not reliably retrieved, reviewed, connected, or promoted into action.

## Before

- Notes, links, sources, and ideas can accumulate without a reliable reuse path.
- Search, learning, and feature prioritization can become disconnected.
- Useful source material can remain private and hard to summarize safely.

## After

- Capture, ingestion, retrieval, and review are treated as one workflow.
- Feature queues and lifecycle states make idea promotion more deliberate.
- Project evidence can be summarized without exposing private notes.
