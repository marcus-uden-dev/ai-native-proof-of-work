# PKM Product Strategy

Last updated: 2026-05-11
Status: Supporting project hypothesis / Internal evidence summarized

## Project Scope

Project: PKM.

Role in portfolio: supporting proof point for knowledge workflow design.

## Target User

Initial target user: builders, operators, and knowledge workers with many notes, links, source fragments, and reusable ideas.

## Core Problem

Useful knowledge material can be captured without becoming searchable, reviewable, learnable, or actionable.

## Existing Alternatives

| Alternative | Gap |
|---|---|
| Notes apps | Store material but often leave retrieval and review to the user |
| Read-it-later tools | Save content but do not reliably turn it into reusable knowledge |
| Generic search | Retrieves documents but does not manage learning or prioritization |
| Task/project tools | Track work but do not preserve source quality |

## Product Wedge

Start with ingestion plus retrieval plus learning/review loops.

## MVP Scope

- Capture and ingestion
- Search and retrieval
- Learning/review surfaces
- Source/person/topic organization
- Browser capture
- Feature queue and prioritization workflow

## Non-MVP Scope

- Public social product
- Team knowledge base
- Claims of measured learning outcomes
- Fully automated publishing

## Feature Prioritization

| Feature | User Value | Complexity | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ingestion | High | Medium | High |
| Search and retrieval | High | Medium | High |
| Learning/review surfaces | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| Browser capture | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| Feed/social concepts | Unknown | Medium | Deferred |

## Risks

- Daily-use adoption risk
- Search quality risk
- Source quality risk
- Operational setup risk
- Private-note exposure risk
