# PKM Competitive Positioning

Last updated: 2026-05-11
Status: Initial hypothesis

## Project Scope

Project: PKM.

Role in portfolio: supporting proof point for knowledge workflow design.

## Positioning Statement

A knowledge workflow product for turning scattered captured material into retrievable, reviewable, and actionable knowledge.

## Differentiation

| Category | Typical Approach | PKM Position |
|---|---|---|
| Notes apps | Store notes and links | Connect capture, ingestion, retrieval, and review |
| Read-it-later tools | Save content | Preserve source quality and promote reuse |
| Search tools | Retrieve documents | Tie retrieval to learning and workflow decisions |
| Project trackers | Track tasks | Connect ideas to feature queues and prioritization |

## What This Is Not

- Not a generic notes archive
- Not a public social network
- Not a claim of measured learning outcomes
- Not a product ready for public launch without operational validation

## Strategic Wedge

The strongest wedge is the capture-to-retrieval-to-review loop: a workflow for making captured knowledge reusable rather than merely stored.
