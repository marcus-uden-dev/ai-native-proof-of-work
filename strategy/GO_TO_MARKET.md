# Go-To-Market Strategy

Last updated: 2026-05-08  
Status: Hypothesis

## Initial Audience

Likely early users:

- job seekers in technical/product/AI-adjacent roles
- career switchers
- consultants
- builders with scattered proof-of-work
- AI-native operators who need a clearer portfolio

## Distribution Channels

| Channel | Why It Might Work | Risk | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn proof-of-work posts | Shows credibility | Requires consistency | Planned |
| Recruiter conversations | Direct relevance | Narrow reach | Planned |
| GitHub portfolio sharing | Strong artifact | Needs polish | Active |
| Reddit launch feedback | Good early validation | Spam risk | Planned |
| Direct outreach | Targeted | Time-consuming | Planned |

## GTM Principle

Do not sell generic AI automation.

Sell:

- clearer execution
- better reusable proof
- reduced workflow friction
- stronger career artifacts
- better decision traceability

## Validation Plan

1. Share private repo with a small number of trusted reviewers.
2. Ask which artifacts are most convincing.
3. Test whether recruiter brief improves conversations.
4. Compare manual CV/portfolio updates against weekly compiler output.
5. Refine positioning based on feedback.
