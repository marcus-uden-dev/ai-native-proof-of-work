# Job-Agent Business Model

Last updated: 2026-05-11
Status: Hypothesis / Not validated

## Project Scope

Project: job-agent.

Role in portfolio: primary proof point and lead career workflow product.

## Possible Customer Segments

| Segment | Problem | Willingness to Pay | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active job seekers | Need faster, better-structured applications | Unknown | Strong initial relevance |
| Career switchers | Need credibility and tailored positioning | Unknown | Strong fit if workflow supports narrative building |
| Freelancers / consultants | Need reusable application and pitch material | Possible | Higher willingness if tied to revenue |
| Career coaches | Need repeatable client workflow support | Possible | B2B2C angle |
| Small talent teams | Need candidate-side workflow insight | Unknown | Later-stage, higher complexity |

## Business Model Options

| Model | Pros | Cons | Best Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free + Pro | Easy adoption | Conversion risk | Individual job seekers |
| Monthly subscription | Predictable revenue | Needs ongoing value and trust | Active job seekers |
| One-time job-search sprint | Clear outcome frame | Less recurring revenue | Career switchers and consultants |
| Usage-based credits | Aligns with application volume | Can feel transactional | Apply support and AI-heavy workflows |
| Coach/team license | Higher revenue | Longer sales and support cycle | Career coaches |

## Current Hypothesis

The most realistic early model is either a one-time job-search sprint package or a lightweight subscription for active job seekers who are applying repeatedly and need workflow continuity.

## Rejected or Deferred Models

| Model | Status | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Fully autonomous job application SaaS | Deferred | Trust, legal, quality, and platform risk |
| Public recruiter-outreach automation | Deferred | Reputation and spam risk |
| Enterprise hiring platform | Deferred | Different buyer and much longer validation cycle |
