## Purpose

These case studies summarize project evidence without exposing private local paths, raw logs, or sensitive details.

Job-agent is the lead proof point. PKM and the household budget app show range across knowledge workflow design and financial-product domain modeling.

Workflow implementation cases are supporting proof points. They show how Claude, Codex, lessons, shared skills, automation, templates, intake flows, and operating logs became repeatable infrastructure around the product work.

Custom skills are documented as sanitized problem/solution cases, not as raw local prompts or private tool files.
