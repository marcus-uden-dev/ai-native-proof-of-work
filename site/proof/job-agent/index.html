  1. 11 May 2026 Origin Problem framing

    Chapter 01

    Before build

    What changed
    Job search was framed as a fragmented, context-heavy workflow rather than only a writing task.
    How it works
    The product scope connects opportunity discovery, career context, application support, research, and follow-up while the applicant retains control.
    Decision annotation
    Build a reviewed workflow, not a generic writer or an autonomous application sender.
    Why it matters
    The product needed to reduce repeated context reconstruction without removing human judgment from sensitive career choices.
    Next question
    Which public product surface can make this connected workflow concrete?

  2. 31 August 2026 v0.9 public proof Discovery system

    Chapter 02

    Set up a repeatable way to find work

    What changed
    Find jobs makes role criteria, sources, saved searches, and monitoring part of one visible discovery surface.
    How it works
    The applicant defines a search, reviews its criteria and sources, then retains a saved route for returning to relevant opportunities.
    Decision annotation
    Make discovery inspectable as a system rather than a single opaque search result.
    Why it matters
    The interface shows how a repeated job-search activity can be organized before it becomes a one-off application task.
    Next question
    How should a discovered role move into a clear next action?

  3. 31 August 2026 v0.9 public proof Daily operating view

    Chapter 03

    Turn the workflow into a daily next move

    What changed
    Today gives the applicant a prioritised view of live work, filters, and the next move in the journey.
    How it works
    The surface combines the current journey position with a selected action and a filtered list of synthetic roles.
    Decision annotation
    Use a daily operating surface to make the next decision visible before asking the applicant to inspect every detail.
    Why it matters
    It makes prioritization visible and gives the product a practical operating rhythm.
    Next question
    What evidence should be visible when the applicant inspects one role?

  4. 31 August 2026 v0.9 public proof Role decision surface

    Chapter 04

    Bring fit, evidence, and action into one role view

    What changed
    Job detail joins role fit, CV gap, company signals, and the next decision in the same reviewable surface.
    How it works
    The applicant can inspect a synthetic role with supporting signals and use that context to decide what to review or prepare next.
    Decision annotation
    Make the decision surface lead the public proof, with discovery and daily context supporting it.
    Why it matters
    A recruiter can assess how the system moves from information to a human-reviewed choice.

Limitations

What this evidence does not prove.

Transparent limits keep interface progress separate from product validation.

  • The release-one company and all company-specific values are synthetic.
  • The prototype has no production service or public user data.
  • Recruiter response, job-search impact, and market outcomes are not measured.
  • The next test evaluates decision clarity and trust, not product-market fit.
