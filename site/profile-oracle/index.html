Hiring brief/Profiles

Evidence views

Start with the role question. Follow the proof.

These role lenses change the reading order, not the evidence. Each capability points to the strongest existing public proof, relevant decisions, and any visible evidence gap.

Evidence boundary

No scores. No inferred strength.

A capability appears here because the repository has an observable decision, product artifact, or explicit evidence boundary. A role lens does not prove every capability in its list.

Mechanisms such as workflow automation, context engineering, and quality gates remain inspectable in the typed taxonomy, but they do not compete with recruiter-facing capabilities.
