Three ways to make the evidence terms easier to scan.

The reading order stays the same. Only the treatment of the evidence terms changes.

01 — Editorial emphasisRecommended when the narrative should stay calm and read like prose.

Role narrative

A product operator who makes AI capability useful in day-to-day work.

The public record gives the strongest support for product framing, workflow design, and AI-native execution. It documents the work through source-linked product decisions, reusable workflow controls, and an evidence trail that can be inspected.

Can Marcus turn business needs into product inputs?

Documented product framing, customer-journey work, and decision records show a repeatable way to turn context into a scoped next action.
